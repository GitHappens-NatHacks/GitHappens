01 / READING COMFORT

A little more room to read.

Digital services should give people time and space to understand information. Long lines, tightly packed words and visual distractions can make a simple task feel complicated.

Reading preferences differ. Try a larger font, adjust the spacing, or follow the reading guide with your mouse. Choose what feels comfortable rather than searching for a single perfect setting.

The extension changes how this page looks. It keeps the words, links and form values. You can jump to the pointer exercise or return to the original appearance with Restore page.

Choose Read → turn on the reading guide → Apply to demo. Then move the mouse across a paragraph.
